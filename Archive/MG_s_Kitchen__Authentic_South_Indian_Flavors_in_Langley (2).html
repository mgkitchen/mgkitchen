1. Event & Service Details

  • Service Scope: Covers drop-off catering service only. Does not include on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management.
  • Delivery Logistics: Dispatched and delivered exclusively via third-party courier (Uber). Client must prepay all round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber delivery charges in full prior to dispatch.
  • On-Demand & Door Setup Labor: Drop-off excludes room staging, food unpacking, chafing dish arrangement, or buffet setup. Pre-agreed door setup or handling is billed at $20.00 per hour.
  • No Discounts Policy: All listed menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable; no discounts or concessions apply.

2. Guest Count & Attendance Guarantees

  • RSVP Cutoff: Client must submit final guaranteed guest headcount between 5 and 10 business days prior to the event date.
  • Billing Floor: Final invoicing is calculated based on the guaranteed minimum headcount submitted at cutoff or actual attendance at the event, whichever is higher.
  • Headcount Reductions: Must be submitted in writing via email at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late reductions cannot be accommodated.
  • Late Additions: Additions exceeding 5 attendees after the initial cutoff incur supplementary per-person charges and depend on food supply availability.

3. Pricing, Invoicing & Payment Terms

  • Immediate & Rush Orders: Orders requested less than 7 days prior require immediate 100% upfront payment upon confirmation and depend strictly on kitchen availability.
  • Grocery & Materials Policy: Under no circumstances will grocery receipts, raw goods, kitchen ingredient invoices, or wholesale food items be transferred or provided to the Client.

5. Food Safety, Allergens & Liabilities

  • Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for delay or failure caused by acts of God, severe weather, transit blockades, utility failures, government restrictions, or unforeseen events beyond control.
  • Allergen & Dietary Requests: Accepted exclusively for the entire order or a 50% volume split. Individual custom allergen plates or separate single-serving packaging cannot be accommodated.
  • Spice Level Specification: Finalized at contract execution; modifications post-signing cannot be guaranteed.
  • Safe Consumption Window: Caterer guarantees quality and food safety up to handover. Client assumes full legal and physical liability for temperature maintenance, hygiene, and safe consumption 2 hours after delivery.
  • Equipment Breakage & Property Damage: Client assumes full financial liability for replacement costs of lost, stolen, or damaged serving utensils, transport carriers, or display rentals.

6. Customizations & Email Communication

  • Written Submission: All menu items, final headcount, spice preferences, and allergen specifications must be submitted in writing.
  • Strict Email-Only Policy: Phone calls, text messages, and verbal discussions are not accepted. All requests, changes, or updates must be emailed to manasagkitchen@gmail.com.
  • Modification Deadline: Any order update must be emailed at least 3 days prior to the event.
  • Last-Minute Menu Changes: Requests past the 3-day deadline depend on ingredient availability and time approval, and incur extra charges.
  • Sweet vs. Non-Sweet Tastings: Sweets cannot be sampled before the event (cannot be made in small test batches). Non-sweet items may be ordered and tasted prior with adjustable spice levels.
