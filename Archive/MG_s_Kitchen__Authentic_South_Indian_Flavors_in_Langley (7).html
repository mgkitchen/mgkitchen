Formal Terms & Conditions · Drop-Off Catering

Catering Service Agreement Rules

Section 1 · Service Scope & Logistics

Drop-Off, Uber & Labor

  • Drop-Off Only: No on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management under standard terms.
  • Prepaid Round-Trip Uber: Delivered exclusively via Uber courier; client must prepay round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber charges prior to kitchen dispatch.
  • Door Setup Labor: Room staging, unpacking, chafing dishes, and buffet setup are excluded. Pre-agreed door setup is billed at $20.00/hr.
  • No Discounts Policy: Menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable.
Section 2 · Guest Count Guarantees

RSVP Cutoff & Billing Floor

  • RSVP Cutoff: Final guaranteed guest headcount must be submitted 5 to 10 business days prior to the event date.
  • Billing Floor: Final invoicing uses the guaranteed minimum headcount or actual attendance, whichever is higher.
  • Reductions: Must be emailed in writing at least 3 business days prior; late reductions cannot be accommodated.
  • Late Additions: Additions exceeding 5 guests post-cutoff incur extra per-person charges subject to supply.
Section 3 · Payment Schedule

50% / 25% / 25% Settlement

  • Initial Deposit: 50% (Non-Refundable) due upon contract execution.
  • Second Installment: 25% due 7 days (1 week) prior to event date.
  • Final Balance: 25% due day of event, prior to order dispatch.
  • Strict Advance Settlement: 100% of food, Uber transit, and labor must clear before dispatch (no COD, deferred, or net terms).
  • Methods: Interac e-Transfer (manasagkitchen@gmail.com) or Credit/Debit Card (merchant surcharges apply).
Section 4 · Cancellations & Rush

Refund Tiers & Grocery Policy

  • 30+ Days Prior: Full refund less minimal administrative and processing charges.
  • 7 to 29 Days Prior: 50% refund, or option to reschedule date/time/venue (subject to capacity & approval).
  • Under 7 Days Prior: 0% refund — total forfeiture of all funds paid.
  • Rush Orders (<7 Days): Require immediate 100% upfront payment upon confirmation.
  • Grocery & Materials Policy: Grocery receipts, raw goods, or wholesale items are never transferred to the Client.
Section 5 · Safety & Allergens

2-Hour Window & Splits

  • Dietary / Allergen Splits: Adjustments accepted exclusively for the entire order or a 50% volume split (no individual single-serving allergen plates).
  • Spice Profile: Finalized at contract signing; post-signing changes cannot be guaranteed.
  • 2-Hour Safe Consumption: Quality guaranteed to handover; Client assumes legal & physical liability for temperature/hygiene 2 hours after delivery.
  • Equipment & Force Majeure: Client liable for lost/damaged rentals; standard Force Majeure exemptions apply.
Section 6 · Communication & Tastings

Email-Only & Sweet Rule

  • Email-Only Policy: Phone calls, texts, and verbal discussions are not accepted for order modifications. All updates must be emailed to manasagkitchen@gmail.com.
  • 3-Day Deadline: Menu changes must be emailed at least 3 days prior; later changes incur extra fees if approved.
  • Sweet Tastings: Sweets cannot be sampled prior to the event (cannot be made in small test batches).
  • Savory Tastings: Non-sweet items may be ordered & tasted in advance with adjustable spice.
