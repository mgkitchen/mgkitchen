Section 1

Event & Service Details

  • Service Scope: Drop-off catering service only. No on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management under standard terms.
  • Delivery Logistics: Dispatched exclusively via third-party courier (Uber). Client must prepay all round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber charges in full prior to dispatch.
  • On-Demand & Door Setup Labor: Drop-off excludes room staging, unpacking, chafing dish arrangement, or buffet setup. Pre-agreed door setup is billed at $20.00/hour.
  • No Discounts Policy: All menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable.
Section 2

Guest Count & Guarantees

  • RSVP Cutoff: Final guaranteed headcount must be submitted between 5 and 10 business days prior to the event date.
  • Billing Floor: Final invoicing is based on the guaranteed minimum headcount or actual attendance, whichever is higher.
  • Headcount Reductions: Must be submitted in writing via email at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late reductions cannot be accommodated.
  • Late Additions: Additions exceeding 5 attendees past cutoff incur supplementary per-person charges subject to supply availability.
Section 3

Pricing & Payment Terms

  • Initial Deposit (50% Non-Refundable): Due upon contract execution.
  • Second Installment (25%): Due 1 week (7 days) prior to the event date.
  • Final Balance (25%): Due on the event day prior to order dispatch.
  • Strict Advance Settlement: 100% of total balance (including Uber fees and labor) must be settled before food leaves the kitchen. No COD or deferred billing.
  • Payment Methods: Interac e-Transfer or Credit/Debit Card (standard merchant processing surcharges apply to cards).
Section 4

Cancellation & Rush Orders

  • 30+ Days Prior: Full refund of monies paid, less minimal administrative and processing charges.
  • 7 to 29 Days Prior: 50% refund issued, or option to reschedule date, time, and venue (subject to kitchen capacity and Caterer approval).
  • Under 7 Days Prior: 0% refund. Total forfeiture of all funds paid.
  • Rush Orders (<7 Days): Require immediate 100% upfront payment upon confirmation and depend strictly on kitchen availability.
  • Grocery & Materials Policy: Under no circumstances will grocery receipts, raw goods, or wholesale ingredient invoices be transferred to the Client.
Section 5

Food Safety & Allergens

  • Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for delays from acts of God, severe weather, transit blockades, utility failures, or government restrictions.
  • Allergen & Dietary Split: Custom dietary or allergen adjustments apply exclusively to the entire order or a 50% volume split—no individual single-serving plates.
  • Spice Specification: Finalized at contract execution; post-signing changes cannot be guaranteed.
  • 2-Hour Safe Consumption Window: Client assumes full legal and physical liability for temperature, hygiene, and safe consumption 2 hours after delivery.
  • Equipment Liability: Client assumes full replacement cost for lost, stolen, or damaged carriers, utensils, or displays.
Section 6

Customizations & Email Policy

  • Written Specifications: All menu items, headcount, spice levels, and allergens must be submitted in writing.
  • Email-Only Communication: Phone calls, text messages, and verbal discussions are not accepted—all updates must be sent strictly via email.
  • Modification Deadline: Changes must be emailed at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late requests depend on availability and incur extra charges.
  • Tastings Policy: Sweets cannot be sampled prior to the event (cannot be made in small test batches). Non-sweet items can be ordered for tasting with adjustable spice levels.
