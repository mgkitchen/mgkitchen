    Formal Terms & Conditions · Drop-Off Catering

    Catering Service Agreement

    This Catering Service Agreement governs drop-off catering services and event delivery between MG's Kitchen — The Flavour Specialist (“Caterer”) and the Client identified in the schedule below.

    Contract Schedule & Event Overview

    Synced with Catering Estimator
    Detail Specification
    Section 1

    Event & Service Details

    • Service Scope: Covers drop-off catering service only. No on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management under standard terms.
    • Delivery Logistics: Dispatched and delivered exclusively via third-party courier (Uber). Client must prepay all round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber delivery charges in full prior to dispatch.
    • On-Demand & Door Setup Labor: Drop-off excludes room staging, food unpacking, chafing dish arrangement, or buffet setup. Pre-agreed door setup or handling is billed at $20.00 per hour.
    • No Discounts Policy: All listed menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable; no discounts, concessions, or reductions apply.
    Section 2

    Guest Count & Attendance Guarantees

    • RSVP Cutoff: Client must submit final guaranteed guest headcount between 5 and 10 business days prior to the agreed event date.
    • Billing Floor: Final invoicing is calculated based on the guaranteed minimum headcount submitted at cutoff or actual event attendance, whichever is higher.
    • Headcount Reductions: Must be submitted in writing via email at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late reductions cannot be accommodated.
    • Late Additions: Guest count additions exceeding 5 attendees after the initial cutoff date incur supplementary per-person charges and depend on food supply availability.
    Section 3

    Pricing, Invoicing & Payment Terms

    Milestone Due Date %
    Initial Deposit Upon contract execution 50% (Non-Refundable)
    Second Installment 7 days (1 week) prior to event 25%
    Final Balance Day of event, prior to dispatch 25%
    • Strict Advance Settlement: 100% of total balance, including Uber transit fees and scheduled labor, must be settled before food leaves the kitchen. No late payments, deferred billing, net terms, or cash-on-delivery.
    • Accepted Payment Methods: Interac e-Transfer (send to mgskitchen@gmail.com) or Credit/Debit Card (subject to standard merchant processing surcharges).
    Section 4

    Cancellation, Rescheduling & Rush Orders

    Timeline Refund & Rescheduling Eligibility
    30+ Days Prior Full refund of monies paid, less minimal administrative & processing charges.
    7 to 29 Days Prior 50% refund issued, or option to reschedule date, time, and venue (subject to kitchen capacity & approval).
    Under 7 Days Prior 0% refund. Total forfeiture of all funds paid.
    • Immediate & Rush Orders: Orders requested less than 7 days prior require immediate 100% upfront payment upon confirmation and depend strictly on kitchen availability.
    • Grocery & Materials Policy: Under no circumstances—including cancellations, partial refunds, or rescheduling—will grocery receipts, raw goods, kitchen ingredient invoices, or wholesale food items be transferred or provided to the Client.
    Section 5

    Food Safety, Allergens & Liabilities

    • Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by acts of God, extreme severe weather, transit blockades, public utility failures, government restrictions, or unforeseen events beyond reasonable control.
    • Allergen & Dietary Requests: Custom dietary, religious, or allergen adjustments are accepted exclusively for the entire order or a 50% volume split. Individual single-serving allergen plates or separate packaging cannot be accommodated.
    • Spice Level Specification: Finalized at contract execution; modifications post-signing cannot be guaranteed.
    • Safe Consumption Window: Caterer guarantees quality and food safety up to handover. Client assumes full legal and physical liability for temperature maintenance, hygiene, and safe consumption 2 hours after delivery.
    • Equipment & Property Damage: Client assumes full financial liability for replacement costs of any lost, stolen, or damaged serving utensils, transport carriers, or display rentals.
    Section 6

    Menu Items, Customizations & Communication

    • Menu & Headcount Details: All menu items, final headcount, spice preferences, and allergen specifications must be submitted in writing.
    • Communication Policy: Phone calls, text messages, and verbal discussions are not accepted. All requests, changes, or updates must be submitted strictly via email.
    • Modification Deadline: Change requests must be emailed at least 3 days prior to the event.
    • Last-Minute Menu Changes: Requests past the 3-day deadline depend on ingredient availability and time approval, and will incur extra charges.
    • Sweet Tastings: Sweets cannot be sampled before the event because they cannot be prepared in small test batches.
    • Food Tastings & Customization: Non-sweet menu items are available for ordering and tasting prior to the event, with adjustable spice levels.
