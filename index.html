Catering Service Agreement

This Catering Service Agreement governs drop-off catering services and event delivery between MG's Kitchen — The Flavour Specialist (“Caterer”) and the Client identified in the schedule below.

Event Specifications & Contract Schedule Inputs

Complete your event details below to populate the Contract Schedule and email your binding specifications to manasagkitchen@gmail.com. All listed menu rates, Uber courier charges, and $20.00/hr labor fees are strictly non-negotiable.

Contract Schedule & Event Overview

Detail Specification
Section 1

Event & Service Details

  • Service Scope: Covers drop-off catering service only. No on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management under standard terms.
  • Delivery Logistics: Dispatched exclusively via third-party courier (Uber). Client must prepay all round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber delivery charges in full prior to dispatch.
  • On-Demand & Door Setup Labor: Drop-off excludes room staging, unpacking, chafing dish arrangement, or buffet setup. Pre-agreed door setup or handling is billed at $20.00 per hour.
  • No Discounts Policy: All listed menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable; no discounts or reductions apply.
Section 2

Guest Count & Attendance Guarantees

  • RSVP Cutoff: Client must submit final guaranteed guest headcount between 5 and 10 business days prior to the event date.
  • Billing Floor: Final invoicing is calculated based on the guaranteed minimum headcount submitted at cutoff or actual event attendance, whichever is higher.
  • Headcount Reductions: Must be submitted in writing via email at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late reductions cannot be accommodated.
  • Late Additions: Guest additions exceeding 5 attendees after cutoff incur supplementary per-person charges and depend on food supply availability.
Section 3

Pricing, Invoicing & Payment Terms

Milestone Due Date Percentage
Initial Deposit Upon contract execution 50% (Non-Refundable)
Second Installment 7 days (1 week) prior to event 25%
Final Balance Day of event, prior to dispatch 25%
  • Strict Advance Settlement: 100% of total balance, including Uber transit fees and scheduled labor, must be settled before food leaves the kitchen. No deferred billing, net terms, or cash-on-delivery.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: Interac e-Transfer or Credit/Debit Card (standard merchant processing surcharges apply to card transactions).
Section 4

Cancellation, Rescheduling & Rush Orders

Timeline Refund & Rescheduling Eligibility
30+ Days Prior Full refund of monies paid, less minimal administrative & processing charges.
7 to 29 Days Prior 50% refund issued, or option to reschedule date, time, and venue (subject to kitchen capacity & approval).
Under 7 Days Prior 0% refund. Total forfeiture of all funds paid.
  • Immediate & Rush Orders: Requested less than 7 days prior require immediate 100% upfront payment upon confirmation, subject strictly to kitchen availability.
  • Grocery & Materials Policy: Under no circumstances will grocery receipts, raw goods, kitchen ingredient invoices, or wholesale food items be transferred to the Client.
Section 5

Food Safety, Allergens & Liabilities

  • Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for delay or failure arising from acts of God, extreme weather, transit blockades, utility failures, or government restrictions.
  • Allergen & Dietary Requests: Accepted exclusively for the entire order or a 50% volume split. Individual custom allergen plates or separate single-serving packaging cannot be accommodated.
  • Spice Level Specification: Finalized at contract execution; post-signing modifications cannot be guaranteed.
  • Safe Consumption Window: Caterer guarantees food safety up to handover. Client assumes full legal and physical liability for temperature, hygiene, and safe consumption 2 hours after delivery.
  • Equipment Breakage & Damage: Client assumes full replacement liability for lost, stolen, or damaged serving utensils, transport carriers, or display rentals.
Section 6

Menu Items, Customizations & Communication

  • Menu & Headcount Details: All menu items, final headcount, spice preferences, and allergen specifications must be submitted in writing.
  • Communication Policy: Phone calls, text messages, and verbal discussions are not accepted. All requests, changes, or updates must be submitted strictly via email.
  • Modification Deadline: Change requests must be emailed at least 3 days prior to the event.
  • Last-Minute Menu Changes: Requests past the 3-day deadline depend on ingredient availability and time approval, and will incur extra charges.
  • Sweet Tastings: Sweets cannot be sampled before the event because they cannot be prepared in small test batches.
  • Food Tastings & Customization: Non-sweet menu items are available for ordering and tasting prior to the event, with adjustable spice levels.
