Section 1

Event & Service Details

  • Service Scope: Drop-off catering service only. No on-site waitstaff, table service, or ongoing event management under standard terms.
  • Delivery Logistics: Dispatched and delivered exclusively via third-party courier (Uber). Client must prepay all round-trip (to-and-fro) Uber delivery charges in full prior to order dispatch.
  • On-Demand & Door Setup Labor: Drop-off excludes room staging, food unpacking, chafing dish arrangement, or buffet setup. Pre-agreed door setup or handling is billed at $20.00 per hour.
  • No Discounts Policy: All listed menu rates, delivery fees, and labor charges are strictly non-negotiable; no discounts, concessions, or reductions apply.
Section 2

Guest Count & Attendance Guarantees

  • RSVP Cutoff: Client must submit final guaranteed guest headcount between 5 and 10 business days prior to the agreed event date.
  • Billing Floor: Final invoicing is based on the guaranteed minimum headcount submitted at cutoff or actual event attendance, whichever is higher.
  • Headcount Reductions: Must be submitted in writing via email at least 3 business days prior to the event. Late reductions cannot be accommodated.
  • Late Additions: Additions exceeding 5 attendees after the cutoff incur supplementary per-person charges and depend on food supply availability.

Section 5

Food Safety, Allergens & Liabilities

  • Force Majeure: Neither party is liable for delays caused by acts of God, extreme severe weather, transit blockades, public utility failures, government restrictions, or events beyond reasonable control.
  • Allergen & Dietary Requests: Custom dietary, religious, or allergen adjustments are accepted exclusively for the entire order or a 50% volume split. Individual single-serving allergen plates or separate packaging cannot be accommodated.
  • Spice Level Specification: Finalized at contract execution; post-signing changes cannot be guaranteed.
  • Safe Consumption Window: Caterer guarantees quality and food safety up to handover. Client assumes full legal and physical liability for temperature maintenance, hygiene, and safe consumption 2 hours after delivery.
  • Equipment & Property Damage: Client assumes full financial liability for replacement costs of any lost, stolen, or damaged utensils, transport carriers, or display rentals.
Section 6

Menu Items, Customizations & Communication

  • Menu & Headcount Details: All menu items, final headcount, spice preferences, and allergen specifications must be submitted in writing.
  • Communication Policy: Phone calls, text messages, and verbal discussions are not accepted. All requests, changes, or updates must be submitted strictly via email.
  • Modification Deadline: Change requests must be emailed at least 3 days prior to the event.
  • Last-Minute Menu Changes: Requests past the 3-day deadline depend on ingredient availability and time approval, and will incur extra charges.
  • Sweet Tastings: Sweets cannot be sampled before the event because they cannot be prepared in small test batches.
  • Food Tastings & Customization: Non-sweet menu items are available for ordering and tasting prior to the event, with adjustable spice levels.
